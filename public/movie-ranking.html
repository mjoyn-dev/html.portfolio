The Best Movies According to Joy

My top 3 movies of all-time

The Notebook 2004

It is a movie that has the confidence to be a love story, pure and simple, and to rely on it's actors to make it something more.

The Greatest Showman

Positions itself as a story celebrating diversity, and the importance of embracing all kinds.

Forgotten Island

Addresses the bittersweet reality that close childhood friendships have a lifespan-making it a devastating yet survivable part of growing up
